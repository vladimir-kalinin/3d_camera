# Privacy Policy

_Last updated: October 15, 2026_

Stereo Camera (`io.github.vladimir_kalinin.stereocamera`) is an open-source camera app developed by
Vladimir Kalinin. This policy explains what data the app accesses and what it does with it.

## Summary

The app does not collect, store on any server, share or sell any personal data.
It has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no internet access.

## Permissions

- **Camera** — to show the preview and take stereo photos and videos.
- **Microphone** — to record sound for videos. Audio is recorded only while video recording is in progress.

Photos and videos are saved only on your device, in the `DCIM/3D Camera` album.
They never leave the device unless you share them yourself.

## Data stored on the device

The app keeps its settings (camera alignment, preview mode, video format) in the app's private storage.
Android may include these settings in your device backup according to your system backup settings.
Uninstalling the app removes them.

## Children

The app does not knowingly collect any data from anyone, including children.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published at this address.
